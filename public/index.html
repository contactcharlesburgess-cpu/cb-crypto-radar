Explain these numbers

Funding: what longs pay shorts (or vice versa) each hour on the perp. Positive = longs pay, which usually means more traders are long.

Open interest: total size of open perp positions. Rising OI with rising price usually means new longs opening; rising OI with falling price usually means new shorts.

Typical daily move (ATR): the average size of one day's range over 14 days. A limit that's 1 ATR below price is about one normal day's drop away.
